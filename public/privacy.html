Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Overview

Authentic Community ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, and safeguards your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, including your name, email address, profile photo, bio, selected interests, and values. Location data is collected only with your explicit permission to recommend local events and nearby community members.

3. How We Use Information

We use your information solely to facilitate authentic community matching, event participation, and secure direct messaging. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest using industry standard protocols via Supabase infrastructure with strict Row-Level Security policies.

5. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You can edit or delete your account and all associated personal data directly within the mobile application settings at any time.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions, contact us at support@authenticcommunity.fun.
