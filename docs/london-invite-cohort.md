# London Invite-Only Cohort — 50 Seats

This first cohort is deliberately small, balanced, and local. Add only people who have opted in to hear about Authentic Community; do not upload or message contacts without their permission.

| Cohort | Seats | Best fit | First destination |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| New in London | 12 | New residents, international students, and people rebuilding a social circle | New in the City + Newcomer Coffee & Conversation |
| Outdoor & wellbeing | 10 | Hikers, runners, meditation, yoga, and weekend-walk regulars | Outdoors / Wellness communities |
| Photography & arts | 8 | Amateur photographers, visual artists, museum and film lovers | Photography / Arts communities |
| Builders & tech | 8 | Early-career developers, designers, founders, and AI-curious members | Tech / AI community |
| Food & culture | 6 | Coffee, cooking, restaurant, language, and cultural-event regulars | Food & Drink / Social communities |
| Community connectors | 6 | Event hosts, volunteer coordinators, and trusted community organisers | Host feedback circle + seeded events |

## Acceptance checks

1. Lives in London or expects to attend London events in the next 30 days.
2. Has chosen at least two interest tags that map to a seeded community.
3. Receives a personal referral or has explicitly opted in.
4. Gets one clear first action: complete profile, join one community, and RSVP to one event.
5. Keep 10 seats in reserve until the first 40 invitees show healthy activation.

## Cohort success targets

- 35+ completed profiles
- 28+ first community joins
- 18+ first messages or stories
- 15+ event RSVPs
- 25%+ day-7 return rate
